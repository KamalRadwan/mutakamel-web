### 1.2 Entry into the Two-Bar Shell (`docs/design/shell.md`)

Accounting inhabits the exact two-bar Tenant Portal shell without introducing a persistent left sidebar on desktop (`>= 1024px` / `lg`):

1. **Bar 1 — `GlobalNav` (`h-[45px]` Ocean Navy `#062E52` + `3px` brand accent strip):**
   - **Start slot (`AppSwitcher`):** Lists `مساحة العمل` / `Workspace`, `إدارة العملاء` / `CRM`, `التجارة` / `Trade`, and — when the tenant is entitled to `accounting.ledger.core` — `المحاسبة` / `Accounting` (`Calculator` icon). Per `10_Feature_Tiers.md` §11, when the tenant lacks `accounting.ledger.core`, the `Accounting` entry and all of its sections are **absent** (never greyed out). Selecting `Accounting` calls `writeStoredApp("accounting")` and navigates to `firstHref(target.sections)` (`/accounting` when accessible, otherwise the user's first accessible item in `ACCOUNTING_NAV_SECTIONS`).
   - **Desktop section triggers (`lg:flex`):** Renders only sections where `section.app === "accounting"` and `section.items.length > 0` after `useNavTree()` filtering. The six top-level section menus (beside the `Overview` home link) follow the accountant's operational loop (`D-175`, `05_Flows/00_Overview.md` §2):
     1. **Overview link:** `نظرة عامة` / `Overview` (`/accounting`, `globalNavDisplay: "items"`, direct link).
     2. **`inbox` menu:** `الوارد` / `Inbox` (full heading: `صندوق الوارد والاستثناءات` / `Inbox and exceptions`).
     3. **`post` menu:** `الترحيل` / `Post` (full heading: `الترحيل والعمليات اليومية` / `Post`).
     4. **`reconcile` menu:** `التسوية` / `Reconcile` (full heading: `التسوية والمطابقة` / `Reconcile`).
     5. **`close` menu:** `الإقفال` / `Close` (full heading: `الإقفال والقوائم المالية` / `Close`).
     6. **`file` menu:** `الإقرارات` / `File` (full heading: `الإقرارات وتقديم المستندات` / `File`).
     7. **`setup` menu:** `الإعدادات` / `Setup` (full heading: `الإعدادات والتأسيس` / `Setup`).
     - Per `GlobalNav.tsx`, if permission/entitlement filtering reduces a section to **1 visible item**, `GlobalNav` renders that item as a direct link instead of a single-item dropdown; if **0 visible items** remain, the section trigger is omitted completely.
     - **In-dropdown group labels (`post` and `setup` only):** While keeping the six top-level sections unchanged, `Post` and `Setup` organize their items under non-interactive bilingual group headings (`DropdownMenuLabel` + `DropdownMenuSeparator` via optional `NavItem.groupLabelKey` in `NavMenu` on `>= lg`, and non-interactive group sub-headings in `NavSheet` on `< lg`). A group heading renders only when at least one item in that group remains visible after `useNavTree()` filtering.
   - **End utility cluster:** `NavCommandPalette` (`Ctrl/Cmd+K`, searching all entitled & permitted items across all apps via unscoped `useNavTree()`), language toggle (`AR`/`EN` with logical RTL/LTR mirroring), theme toggle (`dark`/`light`), and user menu.
   - **Below `lg` (`< 1024px`):** `NavSheet` (`SheetContent side="start"`, opening from right in RTL Arabic and left in LTR English) renders the active app's sections under their full descriptive `labelKey` headings, with an inline app-switcher strip at the top of the sheet.

2. **Bar 2 — `PageActionBar` (`48px` floating chrome bar):**
   - On first-level list screens (`primaryPage: true`), `PageActionBar` suppresses redundant section/screen breadcrumbs and places **at most one filled `primary` CTA** on the trailing (`end`) edge (for example, `مسودة قيد جديدة` / `New journal draft` on `/accounting/post/journal-drafts`).
   - On `[id]` detail screens (for example, `/accounting/post/journal-drafts/[id]`), `PageActionBar` renders the back-location link to the parent list route on the `start` edge and contextual workflow actions (`اعتماد` / `Approve`, `ترحيل` / `Post`) on the `end` edge.

3. **Accounting Operating Context Bar (`AccountingScopeBar` in `src/app/(tenant)/accounting/layout.tsx`):**
   - Mirrors `TradeScopeBar` (`FE-01`) directly below `GlobalNav` for all `/accounting/*` routes:
     - **Company selector (mandatory on `COMPANY`, `COMPANY_OR_BRANCH`, `BRANCH`, `LEDGER`, and `OPERATING_CONTEXT` routes):** Binds `x-mutakamel-company-id` from `user.accessibleCompanies` (persisted in URL/session scope; changing company resets branch/ledger and invalidates React Query `['accounting', tenantId, companyId, ...]`).
     - **Branch selector (optional filter on `COMPANY_OR_BRANCH`; mandatory on `BRANCH`):** Binds `x-branch-id` from `user.accessibleBranchCompanies` filtered to the selected company.
     - **Ledger selector (hidden in Basic):** Rendered **only** when `user.featureEntitlements["accounting.ledger.multi_ledger"] === true` (Wave 2); in Basic, the company's primary statutory ledger is implicit (`10_Feature_Tiers.md` §11).
   - Also hosts the persistent **`RECOVERY_MODE` banner** whenever an API response or `GET /api/tenant/core/v1/restore-incidents/current` reports an active restore incident (`12_Platform_Changes.md` §4.10.2, §10.1).

4. **Home Measures on `/accounting` (`05_Flows/00_Overview.md` §3, `D-175`):**
   - `/accounting` is the operational loop cockpit (not a chart gallery). It surfaces the two home measures defined in `05_Flows/00_Overview.md` §3:
     1. **Count and age of unresolved exceptions** across `GET /accounting/exception-board`, `GET /accounting/posting-exceptions`, and upcoming obligations in `GET /accounting/compliance-calendar` (with active incident callout from `GET /api/tenant/core/v1/restore-incidents/current` and setup readiness from `GET /accounting/company-profile/readiness`).
     2. **Time from document arrival to posted** *(flagged in Contract Gaps — no endpoint in `02_Endpoint_Catalogue.md` §3–§12 currently computes or returns arrival-to-posted latency)*.

### 1.3 How the `tenant_app` Cookie Rule Picks Accounting

`NAV_APP_COOKIE = "tenant_app"` (`src/design-system/shell/nav-app.ts`) coordinates SSR and client navigation without hydration flash:

| Step | Input State | Resolution Rule (`appForPath(pathname) ?? preferred`) | Resulting Active App & Cookie Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Server render (SSR)** | Request arrives at `src/app/(tenant)/layout.tsx` | `isNavAppId(value)` widens to accept `"accounting"`. `storedAppOrDefault(cookies().get("tenant_app")?.value)` passes `initialApp` to `<AppShell>`. | Zero hydration mismatch: an accountant refreshing any page renders Accounting's `GlobalNav` on the first HTML byte. |
| **2. Allowlisted Accounting URL** | `pathname` is `/accounting` or any allowlisted `/accounting/...` route (`isSupportedAccountingPath(pathname) === true`) | `appForPath(pathname)` checks `if (isSupportedAccountingPath(pathname)) return "accounting";` before returning `null`. Route wins over cookie. | Active app is `"accounting"`, even if `tenant_app` held `"workspace"` (deep links and bookmarks always land in Accounting's shell). |
| **3. Unallowlisted `/accounting/*` URL** | `pathname` starts with `/accounting/` but fails `isSupportedAccountingPath(pathname)` (e.g. unshipped Wave 1 route `/accounting/close/budgets` or typo) | `src/proxy.ts` (`resolveModule` widened with `/^\/accounting(?:\/|$)/i`) intercepts before render: `GET`/`HEAD` -> `307` redirect to `/unavailable?module=accounting`; mutating verbs -> `405 ALLOW: GET, HEAD`. `appForPath` returns `null`. | Never renders a dead route or mutates `tenant_app`; `/unavailable?module=accounting` renders inside whatever app `tenant_app` already holds. |
| **4. App-less shared URL** | `pathname` is `/`, `/search`, `/getting-started`, or `/unavailable` | `appForPath(pathname)` returns `null`; `useActiveApp` falls back to `preferred` (seeded from `tenant_app` cookie). | If `tenant_app=accounting`, opening `/search` or `/getting-started` keeps Accounting's `GlobalNav` active (`docs/design/shell.md`). |
| **5. Explicit `AppSwitcher` click** | User selects `المحاسبة` / `Accounting` in `AppSwitcher` or `NavSheet` | `selectApp("accounting")` writes `document.cookie = "tenant_app=accounting; path=/; max-age=31536000; samesite=lax"`, updates React state, and `router.push(firstHref(target.sections))`. | Persists `"accounting"` for 1 year (`SameSite=Lax`, non-secret UI preference) and navigates to `/accounting` (or first accessible route). |
| **6. Entitlement / permission loss** | `tenant_app=accounting`, but tenant loses `accounting` entitlement or user loses all `accounting.*` read permissions | `useNavTree()` filters all Accounting sections to empty. In `AppSwitcher`, if `user.featureEntitlements["accounting.ledger.core"] !== true`, `"accounting"` is **omitted** (`10_Feature_Tiers.md` §11 — absent, never greyed) and `useActiveApp` falls back to `DEFAULT_NAV_APP` (`"workspace"`). | Unentitled tenants never see a greyed Accounting switcher entry or section trigger. |

### 1.4 Two-Gate Navigation Predicate (`useNavTree.ts` + `tenant-routes.ts`)

`TenantUserProfile` (`src/context/AuthContext.tsx`) already carries both `permissions: string[]` and `featureEntitlements: Record<string, boolean>` from Core `/auth/me`, and `useNavTree.ts` already invokes `item.hasAccess(permissions, isTenantOwner, user?.featureEntitlements)`. Every Accounting `NavItem` evaluates:

1. **Entitlement gate (outer):** `featureEntitlements?.[item.featureKey] === true` — if `false` or absent, `hasAccess` returns `false` and the item is **absent** from `GlobalNav`, `NavSheet`, and `NavCommandPalette` (`Ctrl/Cmd+K`), even for `isTenantOwner`.
2. **Permission gate (inner):** `permissions.includes(item.readPermission)` (or `isTenantOwner` on Core `/restore-incidents` where `TenantOwnerGuard` applies alongside `accounting.recovery_incident.approve_correction`). Note that in Accounting (`05_Permissions_and_Features.md` §3), `isTenantOwner` receives permissions via seeded role assignment rather than bypassing missing permission keys or SoD maker-checker checks.
