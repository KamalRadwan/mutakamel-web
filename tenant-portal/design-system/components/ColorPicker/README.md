A free-colour input (`ui-color-picker`) paired with `ui-field`. Every colour input in the product uses `ui-color-picker`: never a raw hex text box, never `<input type=color>` (fixed-palette theme-label pickers such as card colour labels in `shared/ui/actions-menu` stay as they are). Always white `surface-field` in view and edit modes, with a 16px swatch (checkerboard behind translucent values), LTR tabular hex text, and a 14px chevron; keep the label-line `"!"` hint (`tabindex="-1"`). Opens a 248px B1 popover with a ~200×140 saturation/value square, a 14px vertical hue bar (right in LTR, left in RTL), an optional checkerboard opacity slider (`allowAlpha`), a `HEX · RGB · HSL` segmented toggle with channel and `%` inputs, and palette swatches (theme brand/neutral defaults, plus a "No colour" `null` swatch when `nullable`). Applies live with no footer: `Escape` reverts to the open-time colour, outside click commits, and invalid text shows the field error while keeping the last valid value.
